maqueta

A living model railway of Finland. Every train you see is real — positions, delays and rolling stock stream in from Fintraffic's Digitraffic API every 15 seconds.

The layout compresses the country like a real model does; the sun, dusk and weather match Finland's actual sky. Night trains glow. Late trains run late.

How to play: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a train to inspect it, FOLLOW chases it around the layout, LATEST jumps to the most delayed service. esc closes.
